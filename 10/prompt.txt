# V2 Negotiator: post-D01 architecture review and development roadmap

Date: 11 October 2026 (Europe/Zurich). Status: **planning for review; no implementation or experiment authorization**.

## A. Executive assessment

V2 is a usable, bounded decision pipeline with a real, narrowly authorized Arena bridge. It is not yet a demonstrated intelligent hosted negotiator or the complete durable runtime described in the original architecture. Preserve the evidence resolver, official economics, citation admission, independent validation, templates, journals, Lab records and V1. Fix lifecycle ownership before expanding strategy or supplier concurrency.

The first hosted D01 established that one authorized session could be created, its actual history and quote ingested, two real inference requests accounted for, an unsent model package independently evaluated and validated, and one immutable failed run published and retrieved. It established **neither successful model interpretation nor a single buyer submission**. Its score of zero is an execution outcome, not evidence against the strategy's negotiated performance.

Three demonstrated problems dominate:

1. Inference timeout/cancellation and dispatch eligibility have inconsistent ownership. A cancellation race spent a second request; stopping inference then stopped even validated action dispatch. The synchronous-stop patch closes the observed race but does not establish an operational fallback path.
2. Commercial reasoning lacks explicit reference terms and checked effects. The proposer described payment in 45 rather than 30 days as helping supplier cash flow. A valid package and an existing citation do not establish correct reciprocity.
3. Final publication preceded authoritative termination. Remote D01 contains `terminal_json: "null"`; the later three-timeout withdrawal is local only. Terminal capture has been patched, but interruption, transient read failure and uncertain publication remain to be verified.

Recommended target: one decision budget owner; inference availability separate from execution safety; a compact commercial-effects representation over existing assessment/state; bounded LLM strategy with code-derived economic feedback; independent final validation and closing; a durable terminal-evidence/publication barrier. Keep sequential inference as the repair baseline. Evaluate a combined interpretation/proposal call as a treatment after the baseline is measurable; logical module boundaries need not imply separate network requests.

Critical path: lifecycle repair and terminal barrier → commercial-effects/provenance checks → authorized small real-model probes plus integrated offline fault tests → fresh authorization for one controlled D01 → explicit capability review → D02. Do not adapt the predecessor gate simply to admit the failed D01.

### C2. Answers to the architectural questions

1. **Boundaries:** retain evidence, economics, strategy, validation, dispatch and publication ownership. Move budget/failure decisions out of scattered consumers into one owner.
2. **Expensive abstractions:** avoid requiring the original entire event-store/DTO plan before a bounded D01. Per-call JSON roundtrips and repeated official evaluation are possible overhead, not demonstrated bottlenecks; profile first. Do not remove independent final recomputation.
3. **Redundant inference:** the adaptive arm has two sequential calls over substantially overlapping conversation. It has no third selector call. Sequential dependency is real; blind parallel interpretation/proposal would make proposal independent of the new assessment.
4. **Coupling:** `transport.stopped` leaks inference policy into dispatch; fixture construction owns hosted defaults; `prior_evaluations` is free-form strings; coordinator is named offline although used live. Correct these seams without renaming/reorganizing the entire tree.
5. **State:** existing `SupplierAssessment` observations/reported constraints/hypotheses/uncertainties are enough for beliefs. `StrategicState` already has attempts/refusals/questions/feedback, but bridge persistence and updates are incomplete. Add compact effects and strategy memory only where consumed.
6. **Replaceability:** use existing proposal/selection ports plus a small registered bundle choice. A substitute must consume identical evidence/candidates and produce the same nonbinding intents/selection IDs.
7. **Fair comparison:** possible after fingerprints, source isolation, frozen inputs, equal budgets and all-failure denominators are enforced. Current same-observation baseline is a diagnostic, not a hosted treatment comparison.
8. **Grounding:** source/citation existence and independent arithmetic are strong. They do not prove semantic support, complete supplier costs or rationale consistency. Add these distinctions to admission metrics.
9. **D02:** one-part multi-supplier mechanics already exist in `online.py`: economic slices, all-history round reconciliation, supplier memory, rival observed packages and global closing. Durable supplier isolation, fair scheduling and allocation scope need work, not a rewrite. Unknown per-thread timeout attribution currently fails closed.
10. **Advanced methods:** fit existing ports and bounded research service later. Simulation must stay hypothetical; models cannot rewrite evidence, server state, costs or operation ownership.

The current actual flow evaluates inside proposal admission and again after generation, then selects, renders and finally validates. Preserve the legality/cost gate and final validation; introduce feedback earlier through typed reference evaluations. Do not make final validation the sole place where commercial meaning is checked, or select unaudited model terms before economics.

## D. Proposed target architecture

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    O[Owned Arena history and clock] --> E[Scoped evidence and immutable context]
    E --> B[Decision budget and inference admission]
    B --> I[Supplier interpretation]
    I --> N[Existing assessment and supplier state]
    E --> F[Code-derived reference terms and effects]
    F --> S[LLM strategy: package trades and uncertainties]
    N --> S
    S --> C[Legal candidate construction]
    C --> K[Official costs and explicit estimate scenarios]
    K --> A[Selection from approved candidates]
    K -. bounded private feedback .-> S
    A --> R[Template rendering]
    R --> V[Independent fresh-state validation]
    V --> J[Persist exact operation and dispatch permit]
    J --> X[Pre-POST deadline check and dispatch]
    X --> O
    O --> T[Terminal evidence assembly]
    T --> L[Durable single-publication barrier and retrieval proof]
    B --> Q[No-model fallback or recovery required]
    Q --> C
    Q --> V
```

A combined inference treatment implements I and S in one provider exchange, then splits output into independently admitted assessment and proposal artifacts. It does not bypass C, K, A, V or J. An invalid assessment does not silently make a dependent proposal grounded; reject that bundle or mark an explicitly independent proposal and use conservative admission.

### E2. Deadline and cancellation policy

At observation receipt, derive conservative server deadline from request timestamp plus observed seconds. Combine with computation-start deadline, any independently evidenced session/wave cap and authorization lifetime. Map that minimum to a monotonic deadline for the current process; retain UTC/server provenance for restart. Do not fabricate wave evidence if the API supplies only thread clocks.

```text
available_for_advice = min(server, wave-if-known, compute, authorization deadlines) - now
                      - fallback_reserve - submission_reserve - uncertainty_margin
stage allowance = min(configured stage cap, remaining total model time, available_for_advice)
admit call only when stage allowance meets the measured conservative completion requirement
```

Use measured bounded-probe latency (cold and warm), plus refresh/history/limiter/HTTP observations, to set reserves. Small samples provide empirical ranges, not credible p99 estimates. Keep an explicit hard upper cap; do not increase 8/4/12 blindly or reset budgets per call. Trace skipped inference with the remaining window and admission reason. Early fallback preserves time for validation and dispatch; below the submission reserve, return recovery-required/no-send unless a separately admissible close is available.

Synchronously revoke model admission on timeout before any await/return, increment decision generation and quarantine late output. Cancel child tasks; consume exceptions; no late completion may update assessment, selected payload or memory. External caller cancellation revokes execution as well. Ordinary schema failure can choose a documented no-model fallback; timeout with unknown usage retains the entire reservation and prevents another model call in that decision. Ceiling exhaustion disables advice, while authorization/integrity mismatch stops execution.

Existing `OfflineAllowance` reservations remain authoritative for spend ceiling. Ledger timing is authoritative for decision admission. Record reserved and provider-reported actual usage separately, including partial/unknown charges; never refund an unknown request to fund another. Local cancellation is not proof that the provider stopped computing. A reset for a later decision must preserve run-level accounting and explicitly honor the reviewed run-level failure policy.

Bound all outer waits, including cancellation-resistant tasks and loop shutdown. No generic async migration is needed for D01: first prove the current serial bridge bounded under adversarial cleanup, then replace its loop boundary only if necessary. Network phase timeouts do not replace an absolute deadline. Dispatch must check remaining time **after** final history/limiter work and immediately before HTTP, against the persisted exact operation. If no request was issued, record proven-not-dispatched; if it may have been issued, preserve uncertain ownership and reconcile read-only.

### E3. Commercial reasoning and economics feedback

Use the current supplier offer as the commercial comparison reference, not buyer reference payment days or a hidden optimum. Code annotates changed receipt timing and independently evaluates complete buyer costs. The LLM interprets interests, designs the exchange and explains uncertainty.

Require each significant change to identify: what the buyer requests; what the buyer gives; which observed/claimed preference supports the trade; what remains unknown; and how the next supplier response could revise it. An aspirational opening can be buyer-favorable without compensation, but must be labeled a request, not mutual benefit. Contract extension need not be declared compensating merely to make the package pass.

For the D01 package, checked effects must say delayed receipts, reduced unit price, later increase and longer commitment; supplier net value unknown. Reject structured claims that assert earlier receipts or established cash-flow improvement from longer payment alone. Check structured effects, not arbitrary prose with keyword regex. A concise human-reviewed rubric evaluates whether the explanation's meaning is consistent; deterministic validation cannot prove all free-text commercial logic. On contradiction, use the prevalidated simple fallback/clarification if admissible; do not add an unbudgeted repair call.

First supply evaluated reference packages (opening and existing fallback) and typed relative component effects to strategy; then evaluate its candidate. This supplies economic context without an extra inference pass. Optional later `CandidateResearch.evaluate_intents` allows one bounded feedback/revision step on the same snapshot with registered batches, only when funded and time permits. Keep no more than the existing candidate/call ceiling; no open-ended tool loop.

Supplier-cost uncertainty should affect confidence and questions, not produce a deterministic floor. Search explicitly scoped source records for missing economics; retain separate source assumptions and sensitivities. If unavailable, record relationship component unestimated and qualify nominal ranking. A nominally cheapest package is not certified sustainable. For relationship-sensitive comparisons, evaluate available scenarios and show whether ranking changes; do not invent probability weights or default missing full cost to zero.

### E4. Terminal evidence and immutable publication

Use the existing local run snapshots and atomic-file pattern; add a small durable publication journal, not a new remote service:

```text
execution ended/failed → collecting_terminal → evidence_complete
                     ↘ evidence_missing (local, retryable read-only recovery)
evidence_complete → publication_prepared (frozen payload hash + destination + run ID)
                  → write_attempted → verified / rejected / outcome_unknown
```

Save original exception, exact source/config, session and operation identities immediately. Bound terminal collection by total deadline *and* per-read timeout. The current 300-second polling loop can exceed its nominal bound through a 180-second client read; catch/classify transient read failures and clip them to remaining collection time. Collect all owned threads and attachment provenance, verify ended state/result/session consistency, then build the final payload. Preserve run/attempt failed status after server timeout.

No terminal evidence by the bound: mark fields unknown and local publication blocked; do not publish a success or fabricate terminality. Resume collection from the same run/session with read-only tools under the applicable access scope; consumed authorization never becomes permission for a new session. Resume publication only after checking remaining original write authorization or obtaining an explicit new publication-only allowance.

Persist `write_attempted` before the network write. A crash between marker and HTTP creates uncertainty; use GET of that run ID to compare the frozen payload. Matching remote record means verify with no second write. Missing record after an ambiguous PUT does not automatically authorize retry; preserve uncertainty and seek explicit reviewed write permission if needed. Definitive rejection is separate from uncertain network outcome. Retrieval/listing proof is required after acknowledged upload. Never republish or enrich the original D01 record; any later separately authorized finding may cite both the original and local supplement as distinct artifacts.

## G. Validation strategy and metrics

### Five-level ladder

| Level | Scope / cost control | Meaningful checks and exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 deterministic | Existing local dependencies; no external services | Cancellation ordering and complete caller return; deadline/reserve arithmetic; unknown-use accounting; private/supplier provenance; official parity; payload legality; pre-HTTP expiry; immutable publication ordering/recovery |
| 2 real-model probes | Separately authorized inference-only allowance; frozen 24-case plan, one call topology per treatment; no Arena or Lab writes | Payment direction, price concessions, duration benefit uncertainty, cash flow, unsupported supplier costs, relationship priorities, conflicting signals, reciprocal packages. Independent labels; JSON validity reported separately. Extra repetitions/treatments require explicit expanded allowance |
| 3 integrated offline | Recorded hosted history plus synthetic fault timing and official simulator seam; no hosted quota | Freshness, actual selection/evaluation linkage, all fallback outcomes, acknowledgement-based feedback, crash/read/publication fault cases. In-process simulator proves contracts, not network latency or persuasion |
| 4 controlled D01 | One fresh authorized session after lower gates | Admitted actual interpretation, acknowledged proposal, supplier feedback, safe closure/terminality, complete publication. No automatic second attempt |
| 5 D02 | Local supplier-isolation gates then fresh one-session allowance | Multiple histories/preferences, RFQ coverage, executable alternatives, cross-supplier costs, legal award/allocation, adaptation and bounded execution |

Probes should include 15→30, 30→45 and 60→30 payments; unchanged-payment cases; earlier/later price-increase dates; durations with unsupported or explicitly refused commitments; relation language paired with liquidity/approval constraints; false index/full-cost inference; supplier prompt injection. Label strategy acceptable as a range of grounded moves rather than a single expected price. A counteroffer can be strategically valid even if a synthetic supplier declines it.

Real-model latency characterization uses the frozen requests and includes cold/warm connection status, input/schema bytes, output length, task wait and parse/consumer time. No cost or latency claim derives from mocks. Store counts/median/range and censored timeouts; p95/p99 claims need sufficiently many independently funded observations. Preserve failed requests in denominators.

### Metric definitions

| Dimension | Diagnostic measure | Actual outcome / interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Reliability | Admitted calls, fallback/late-result rejection, pending writes, deadline violations, publication completeness | Sessions finishing without infrastructure failure; acknowledged buyer actions; server timeout count; critical violations must be zero |
| Interpretation | Supported observation/constraint precision and recall; contradiction/negation accuracy; source-role confusion | Agreement with independent labels, not model confidence or citation existence alone |
| Commercial reasoning | Term-direction correctness, unsupported economics, explicit give/request/unknowns, prose-effect consistency | Held-out human rubric; no fabricated compensation value or false cash-flow improvement |
| Adaptation | Paired evidence perturbation changes belief/choice appropriately; refusal retained; acknowledgement linkage | Supplier-facing changes justified by actual new information; changing numbers alone is insufficient |
| Validity | Generated/admitted/rejected candidates; fresh legal fields, options, must-haves, certification | Invalid submitted packages and unauthorized closes must be zero |
| Economic quality | Official nominal components; relationship estimate coverage; sensitivity/ranking changes; same-observation counterfactual | Realized official deal cost/score and executable-alternative comparison; no-deal costs unknown unless officially defined |
| Negotiation | Give/request packages, supplier refusals/conditions, number of substantive rounds | Agreement/no-deal, legal allocation, final score; agreement rate alone cannot establish quality |
| Inference cost | Reserved calls/tokens/dollars; known provider tokens; estimated vs reported cost; unknown usage count | Spend bounded by approved reservations; no fabricated total actual spend when usage missing |
| Latency | Context/model/parse/evaluate/refresh/limiter/POST/terminal spans; time remaining at dispatch | Deadline-compliant acknowledged actions; task-level latency does not substitute for complete turn latency |

Freeze fixture/model/prompt/source/config identities and treatment order before comparison. Use common recorded observations for causal diagnostics; use independent hosted sessions for outcome comparisons. Report per-case failures and uncertainty, not only average scores. If provider/supplier caching or stochasticity limits independence, say so. One D01 run proves a capability instance, not reliability rate or superiority. Selection of a more expensive candidate requires a grounded trade hypothesis; its later response tests that hypothesis.

## H. D01 completion criteria

The next authorized D01 establishes first hosted negotiation capability only if all are demonstrated:

1. Fresh reviewed source/config and exact authorization; one owned session; all ceilings and endpoint constraints respected.
2. Genuine interpreter output admitted and independently reviewed for the actual opening. Fallback does not count as admitted interpretation.
3. At least one actual LLM-assisted buyer package with correct commercial directions/qualified reciprocity, official costs and legal validation is acknowledged in authoritative thread history. A validated unsent trace is insufficient.
4. A real supplier response to that buyer action is captured. If another decision is possible, it uses that response and demonstrates justified continuation/change or independently evaluated closing. If early terminality prevents multi-round adaptation, mark it untested and require offline proof plus explicit D02 review disposition; do not invent a second round.
5. Safe terminal result obtained with no unresolved write or unintended automatic buyer timeout. All close paths preserve shared authority and economic comparison. A defensible no-deal can establish execution capability; a favorable agreement additionally requires realized-quality review.
6. Complete original evidence precedes the sole immutable publication; individual retrieval and ordinary listing match the frozen payload. Unknown inference usage remains reserved and explicit.
7. Reviewer distinguishes capability pass from economic-performance verdict and resolves material source/relationship gaps. A score cannot override semantic, safety or evidence failures.

Do not define “successful D01” as accepting the opening offer or reaching any deal. Retain the original failed record and link the new capability proof separately.

## I. D02 entry criteria and predecessor decision

Recommend **A: repair and rerun D01**, retaining original predecessor semantics. Add capability-specific review evidence to clarify readiness, not to manufacture a completed predecessor. Option B (reviewed-failure path) is unnecessary while the required capabilities remain unproved. Option C is attractive longer term if “completed” becomes confused with “agreement”; revise transparently only after concrete capability evidence and review, preserving historical gate decisions.

Current `prior_review` requires a completed predecessor run, exactly one adaptive retrieval proof, genuine model identity, a decision with both interpretation/proposal admitted, and ended sandbox terminal evidence. The failed D01 fails completion and interpretation, regardless of its locally supplemented terminal result. It cannot pass by changing review booleans. A completed, defensible no-deal may satisfy the code gate; section H supplies the additional engineering meaning.

Before D02:

- Section H is satisfied or every deliberately unobserved capability has an explicit, cited review disposition without weakening hard safety/provenance gates.
- Supplier-specific memory survives restart; foreign refs and rival utility beliefs cannot leak into another supplier assessment.
- All eligible histories, standing offers/defaults and actual rounds are available; incomplete timeout attribution yields bounded recovery or a reviewed conservative policy.
- RFQ coverage and serial time allocation are measured; global close compares complete eligible costs and the executable alternative without double counting demand.
- Actual D02 catalog/schema confirms permitted award/volume/allocation behavior. Selecting a single supplier is distinct from split allocation; implement only supported decisions.
- Model projection, buyer secrecy, relationship-cost uncertainty and all-thread dispatch economics pass offline tests.
- Terminal evidence/publication recovery and source/config parity are proven locally; fresh exact D02 authorization covers its own session, inference and write allowance. P10 remains separate.

Future D02 evaluation should measure per-supplier interpretation/response coverage, belief revisions, offered and awarded demand, alternatives considered, actual cost/score, legal allocation, missed deadlines, usage and latency. Do not optimize coverage alone by interviewing every bidder when closing is already economically and legally justified; record skipped coverage and its grounds.

Primary pages were checked during this review. Summaries below are limited to publication abstracts/metadata and the opponent-model survey; no method reproduction or procurement-transfer claim is made. Read full assumptions/code/data/license before implementation.

| Direction / timing | Established source | Our hypothesis and test / rejection condition |
|---|---|---|

| Procurement interpretation labels; after reliability | [Chawla et al., CaSiNo (2021)](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.254/) provides human multi-issue negotiations and persuasion-strategy annotations | Adapt only labels needed for requests/refusals/conditions. | Tool-derived utility feedback; M2 first, bounded revision later | [Oh et al., MERIT (2026)](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1155/) studies utility-centered feedback, metrics and prompting/learning | Typed official component deltas improve buyer-objective alignment while retaining supplier uncertainty. Ablate feedback; reject private-data leakage, fabricated supplier utility or higher compute without improved outcomes |
| Concession planning and one-pass self-critique; after D01 | A bounded strategy hypothesis, not a demonstrated V2 result | In the same call, check give/request balance and prior refusals before returning. | Bayesian preference inference; D02 or later | Baarslag survey covers families of opponent modeling | Only infer distributions when repeated scoped observations support identifiable parameters; combined term changes cannot identify one-field premiums. | Limited dialogue lookahead; longer term | [Lewis et al., Deal or No Deal? (2017)](https://aclanthology.org/D17-1259/) reports dialogue rollouts in hidden-reward multi-issue negotiation | At most a small frozen candidate/response budget offline; compare to equal-compute direct sampling. Reject simulated agreement treated as fact or poor real-response calibration |
| Repeated-opponent adaptation; longer term | [Liu et al., opponent simulation (2026)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/liu26ef.html) uses opponent imitation and best-of-N simulation inspired by smooth fictitious play in repeated games | Does sparse Arena history suffice? | Robust benchmark design and trajectory learning; later | [Bianchi et al., NegotiationArena (2024)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/bianchi24a.html) finds behavioral sensitivity in multiple LLM negotiation settings | Test portability across suppliers/categories rather than exploitation of one bot. Do not claim Nash/Pareto optimality with unknown supplier utility. Relationship scenarios are conditional arithmetic, not known willingness to accept. Start with the minimum qualitative state and economic feedback that resolve D01's observed reasoning failure. Research priority is established by measurable benefit, not sophistication.
