Supplier: Dichtungswerk Ravensteiner GmbH (seal maker, group company, Segment Manager Monika Zellner, inside sales).
Its 18 Sept e-mail: "due to very high capacity utilisation we cannot confirm your full forecast ... We will
prioritise customers with firm, long-term commitments." Its March e-mail: "We can confirm additional volumes only
against a binding 12-month commitment." Price changes above 2 % need its pricing committee (meets every two weeks).
It opens 19890 pcs / 12 months / 54 days at 32.20 (15 % below our need of 23400). It charges a premium for lead
times shorter than its standard (42 days: +1.90; 35 days: +2.90) and declares a FINAL offer at once when the first
sheet deviates from its standard - NEVER ask for 35 or 42 days: always 56 days. In the last session a first sheet
with 36 months plus a request to "confirm ... and agree to the proposed price" also drew an immediate final offer
(32.00). The only first sheet that has ever drawn an OPEN reply is 23400 / 24 months / 56 days (reply 31.60-32.00).
It says "the contract term is reflected in the price": 36 months is worth about 0.40 to it and costs us nothing.
OBSERVED MECHANIC (21 sessions): first reply is a package answer whatever our price. Then it mirrors small (we +1.40,
it -0.20 to -0.40) and declares final on its SECOND reply - nobody ever gave it a new verified claim in round 2.
Hypothesis: start on the one package that keeps it open, extend the commitment to 36 months in round 2 together
with one NEW verified claim and a measured step, and it steps once more instead of going final.
Plan: ROUND 1 - ONE sheet S23 (23400 / 24 months / 56 days) at level L15 (about 27.55). Text: thank it, state that
