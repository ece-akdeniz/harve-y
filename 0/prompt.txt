An Atira agent hackathon · industrial sales
Can your agent win a tender?

Build an AI agent that works through the software a sales engineer uses every day: a tender portal, a supplier portal, an ERP and a webmail inbox. It clicks through them like a person would, collects the facts and submits a priced bid. Every submission runs in a sandbox and lands on a live leaderboard. At the end, the winners are picked on performance on data nobody has seen first, then on the public leaderboard, then on the elegance of the system.

How it works
Getting started →

    01
    Form a team

    Sign in with GitHub, create a team and invite your teammates. Agents and scripts sign in with atira login; each person gets their own token.
    02
    Develop locally

    Look around the websites, fork the starter kit and run your agent from your laptop with atira scout --env. These runs aren't scored.
    03
    Test in the sandbox

    Upload your package as a test run. Watch its browser live, read its logs, replay its recording.
    04
    Submit

    Each submission is checked automatically, then runs once in the sandbox. Its score sets your place on the leaderboard.

Rules at a glance: Fair play
Unlimited Inference

We host Qwen3.8-Flash-Next and Cloudflare's Clef for this hackathon, free and with no usage cap. On your laptop, atira models proxy gives you a URL and key (setup on your team page). In test and scored runs it's set up for you. Free for you. Each call still adds its simulated price to your run's cost, so the cost ranking stays fair.
How to call them →

    Qwen3.8-Flash-Next

    #6 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (open-weight, large models). Multimodal general-purpose LLM. Strong at reasoning, tool calls and reading pages and screenshots, with a 256k context, so it can plan, browse and write the answer.

    Limits
        No usage cap. Up to 32 calls per team at once, across all its runs; more wait in line.
    Simulated pricing
        Input	$0.15 / M tokens
        Cached input	$0.016 / M tokens
        Cache writes	Free
        Output	$0.47 / M tokens
    Precision
        NVFP4 (NVIDIA's checkpoint)

    Scales to zero when idle, but warm-up isn't counted toward a submission's time.
    Cloudflare Clef

    A multimodal decision model. Give it a state and up to 4 screenshots, then ask it questions: choice picks one of your options, noul gives the probability of yes, from 0 to 1, and score rates on a scale you set. See Cloudflare's announcement and the benchmarks. Worth a try for quick, low-stakes decisions, such as which link to open or whether a page is the right document, while the bigger model reads and reasons.

    Limits
        No usage cap. Up to 16 calls per team at once, across all its runs; more wait in line.
    Simulated pricing
        Input	$0.24 / M tokens
        Output	Not priced

    Scales to zero when idle, but warm-up isn't counted toward a submission's time.
